# Startup IQ MVP Scope

## Problem and users

Founders often have an idea but lack a structured way to separate evidence from assumptions and identify what to validate next. Startup IQ is for student entrepreneurs, early-stage and first-time founders, startup mentors, and faculty evaluating startup ideas.

## Solution and workflow

The MVP collects a startup profile, captures evidence and unknowns, applies a deterministic seven-dimension rubric, shows an explainable score and dimension breakdown, identifies evidence gaps, provides rule-based recommendations, and produces a downloadable report. Optional AI can summarize information or draft a business-plan outline, but cannot set the numerical score.

## Included features

- Startup information intake.
- Seven-dimension assessment and weighted scoring.
- Evidence and assumption tracking.
- Results dashboard and dimension breakdown.
- Evidence-gap analysis and rule-based recommendations.
- Optional AI-assisted summarization and business-plan drafting.
- Local persistence and downloadable report.

## Explicit exclusions

Live market data, automatic competitor research, complex financial forecasting, advanced investor-readiness prediction, authentication, collaboration, enterprise administration, advanced analytics, and production cloud infrastructure are deferred.

## Definition of done

The MVP is complete when a user can enter an idea, complete an assessment, view an explainable score, understand missing evidence, receive recommendations, and download a report. The product is an analytical indicator, not a prediction of success or a substitute for investment advice.
